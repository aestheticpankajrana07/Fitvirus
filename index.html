From the forests of Sundargarh to the screens of millions — Somnath Ray, known across the internet as Fitvirus, is a calisthenics athlete, content creator, and one of the most prominent fitness voices to emerge from Western Odisha. His story is not about shortcuts. It is about showing up, every single day.

I
Chapter 01 / Origin

WHERE IT
ALL BEGAN

Sundargarh, Odisha — a land of red earth, dense forests, and iron determination. It was here that Somnath Ray first discovered what the human body could do when pushed beyond comfort. There was no blueprint, no coach, no gym membership initially. Only the raw hunger to be stronger than yesterday.

The forests became his training ground. The open sky, his witness. Every early morning, every aching session — it was all investment in a version of himself the world had not yet seen.

"The jungle doesn't care about your excuses. Neither did I."

II
Chapter 02 / Discipline

THE ART OF
SHOWING UP

Discipline is the difference between a dream and a reality. Somnath built his physique and his calisthenics mastery through relentless consistency — training in every condition, pushing every boundary.

Calisthenics became his language. Planches on poles above mountain valleys. Human flags by the sea. Moves that seem to defy gravity — all earned through thousands of hours of deliberate practice, recovery, and mental fortitude.

"Every rep is a vote for the person you're becoming."

III
Chapter 03 / Rise

THE CAMERA
FOUND HIM

A phone, a gimbal, and a story worth telling. Somnath didn't just decide to be a creator — he decided to document a genuine journey, and the internet responded. The @fitvirus YouTube channel began attracting viewers not with gimmicks, but with authenticity.

From long-form gym content to viral Shorts, each video carries the same signature: real effort, real results, real Odisha. His content became a mirror for the thousands of young fitness enthusiasts across Western India who had never seen themselves represented at this level.

"Don't create for the algorithm. Create for the one person who needs it."

IV
Chapter 04 / Recognition

CERTIFIED
BY THE WORLD

On 07 January 2020, Somnath Ray performed a feat that would be officially recognized by the World Book of Records, United Kingdom — one of the world's most respected record-keeping institutions. The certification was formally issued on 02 July 2020.

This was not just a personal milestone. It was a declaration that from the forests and fields of Sundargarh, world-class athleticism is being born. A message to every young person in Odisha: your geography does not define your ceiling.

"From Sundargarh to the World Book of Records. Every dream has an address."
